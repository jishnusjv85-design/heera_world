Having trouble paying?

Opened from Instagram?
  1. Android: tap ⋮ (top right) → Open in Chrome (or “Open in browser”), then tap Donate Now again.
  2. iPhone: tap ••• (top right) → Open in external browser, then tap Donate Now again.
  3. Or take a screenshot of the QR code and open it with “Scan from gallery” in your UPI app.
On an iPhone?

iPhones don't have one default UPI app, so after you tap Donate Now, choose the app you've installed (Google Pay, PhonePe or Paytm). If none opens, copy the UPI ID and pay from inside your app using “Pay to UPI ID”.

Payment failed or shows a limit error?

Some apps set limits on new payees. Try a smaller amount, or a different UPI app. If money was debited but the app shows failed, banks normally reverse it automatically. Please don't pay twice; contact us first.

How do I know my donation was received?

Your UPI app shows the payment status and a transaction ID (UTR). Keep a screenshot. For a receipt, send the UTR to us on WhatsApp or call +91 99619 02533.

About the 30 Homes Campaign

Heera World Foundation is a registered Section 8 non-profit company based in Ponmala, Malappuram, Kerala. We build homes for poor families and support people facing medical emergencies.

Through the 30 Homes Campaign we are building houses for 30 families who live without a safe, permanent home.
