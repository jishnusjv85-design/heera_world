Having trouble paying?

Copy button didn't work?

Press and hold the UPI ID 8921811355@okbizaxis to select and copy it, or type it into your UPI app.

How do I pay with the saved QR?

Open your UPI app, tap Scan, then the gallery/photo icon, and pick the saved QR image. Enter the amount and pay.

Opened from Instagram and saving doesn't work?

Press and hold the QR image to save it, or take a screenshot. You can also tap ⋮ / ••• (top right) → Open in browser and save from there.

Payment failed or shows a limit error?

Some apps set limits on new payees. Try a smaller amount, or a different UPI app. If money was debited but the app shows failed, banks normally reverse it automatically. Please don't pay twice; contact us first.

How do I know my donation was received?

Your UPI app shows the payment status and a transaction ID (UTR). Keep a screenshot. For a receipt, send the UTR to us on WhatsApp or call +91 99619 02533.
